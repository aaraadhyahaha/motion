# Atelier visual layer (#ui #type #anim)

Owner verdict on Phase 2 UI: colors shabby, app boring. HIG stays the **base** (structure, type ramp, 44pt, springs, light/dark, reduced-motion/-transparency); "Atelier" is the fun layer on top. Chosen from 3 subagent directions (Atelier / Sticker / Almanac): **Atelier + Almanac's mono numerals now; Almanac's time-of-day header tint later.**

Pitch: warm paper, an editorial serif and clay ink — a studio notebook that happens to schedule your day.

## Constraints

- Zero JS growth in the entry chunk (initial 89.69/90 KB). CSS has room (≤ ~2 KB gz added). No new runtime deps.
- Animate only transform/opacity; one-shot animations; grain is a static tile on page/sidebar only (never cards/rows, never animated).
- `--tint` (clay) only for fills, glyphs, ≥15px semibold. Small tinted text → `--tint-ink`.

## 1. Tokens (done — `tokens.css`, `base.css`)

Warm labels/fills/backgrounds/separators, clay `--tint` + `--tint-ink`, brick `--red`, moss `--green`, list inks on legacy `--list-*` names, warm materials/scrim, radii 10/16/24, `--shadow-card`, `--grain` (alpha baked in; `light-dark()` is colour-only), `--font-serif` (Instrument Serif) on `.t-large-title` (50/52) and `.t-title2/3` (25/30, 22/27, 400), `--font-mono` + `.mono`, `.t-eyebrow`.

## 2. Fun layer (component work)

- **Surfaces:** grain on body + sidebar; task groups = cards with `--shadow-card`, no hairline box; row separators 1px dashed, inset.
- **Header:** large serif title, hand-drawn clay swash under it (CSS mask, no JS), then one note line: date + voice ("Thursday, November 26. Four on the desk, one carried over."). The mockup's eyebrow-above-title was dropped in the impeccable polish pass (kicker-above-heading is a craft-floor ban); the date moved into the note. Small inline nav title stays system font.
- **Section counts:** open count after each section title (mono, `--label3`). Sidebar: serif wordmark with clay full stop, serif group headings.
- **List dots:** irregular ink-blob radius, rotated −12°.
- **Checkbox:** stamp — fill scales in with −8° tilt, one clay ring blooms (`scale`+`opacity`, single run), strike-through 1.5px clay.
- **Quick add:** solid paper card, "+" in a 32px clay coin.
- **Tab bar / sidebar:** active item `--tint-ink` + 4px dot under the label; sidebar brand in serif.
- **Section headers:** serif `.t-title3`; Overdue section in `--red`.
- **Times/durations/counts:** `.mono` (Almanac borrow).
- **Empty state:** line-art teacup with clay steam. "Desk's clear." / "Nothing due today. Go make something, or make tea."
- **Voice:** warm, a little wry. Toast "Done. Nicely handled." · overdue hint "Still on your list from Tuesday."
- **Sheet / stats:** sheet radius 24, serif title field, clay segmented controls; stats tiles as cards with serif numbers, heatmap in clay steps, per-list bars in inks.
